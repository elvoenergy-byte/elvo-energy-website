Why check your rebate now

Victoria's Solar Homes Program settings don't stay still for long. Here's what's changed most recently and why it's worth confirming your numbers before you commit to a quote.

The income cap tightening from 1 July 2026 means some households who qualified last year no longer will — and others who assumed they wouldn't qualify might now be under the new threshold depending on how it's calculated. The federal battery discount is also still relatively new, and not every installer quote factors it in correctly. Getting a second, independent check costs nothing and takes a few minutes.

Latest updates

From 1 July 2026

Solar Homes income cap lowered

Combined household income cap for the panel and hot water rebates drops to $150,000/year.

2025 onward

Battery loans replaced

Victoria's interest-free battery loan closed; battery support now runs through the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program instead.

Ongoing

STC value is shrinking

Federal Small-scale Technology Certificates reduce in value each year on the way to the scheme's 2030 end date — earlier is better.
