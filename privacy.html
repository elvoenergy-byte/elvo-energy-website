Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Elvo Energy provides independent marketing, sales and guidance on solar panels, solar batteries, heat pumps and related rebates for Victorian homes and businesses. We do not install systems ourselves. When you ask for a quote or installation, we may pass your enquiry to a local installer partner. This policy explains how we handle your personal information.

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form or chat with Ella, our online assistant, we may collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number;
  • your suburb or postcode;
  • the type of enquiry (home, business or installer partner);
  • your preferred time for a call back;
  • your household income range, only where you choose to give it so we can check rebate eligibility;
  • the messages you send in the chat and any details you give about your property or energy use.

You do not have to give us this information, but we may not be able to help you or check your rebate eligibility without it.

Ella is an automated assistant

Ella is an automated online assistant, not a person. Messages you send to Ella are recorded so our team can follow up with you.

How we use your information

  • to respond to your enquiry and call you back;
  • to check whether you may be eligible for Victorian or federal rebates;
  • to pass your enquiry to an installer partner so they can prepare a quote, where you have asked for one;
  • to improve our website and services.

We only contact you about your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We may share your details with:

  • our installer partners, so they can contact you about your quote or installation;
  • service providers that host our website, process our forms and provide our email. Some of these providers may store information on servers outside Australia.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. Enquiries are sent to a restricted business email account. No system is completely secure, so please do not share information with us that you do not want to share.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the reasons above, then delete it or make it anonymous.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handled your information, contact us first and we will try to fix it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
