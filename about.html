About Elvo Energy

Guidance first, installation second.

Most solar businesses are installers first — which means every conversation eventually points back to their own product. We built Elvo Energy the other way around.

Why we don't install

Elvo Energy handles the marketing, sales guidance and rebate navigation for solar panels, batteries and heat pumps. We don't hold an installation licence ourselves, and we've kept it that way deliberately.

When a business both recommends a system and installs it, it's hard to know whether the recommendation is genuinely the best fit — or just the easiest to sell. Keeping those two roles separate means the advice you get from us isn't shaped by which product is sitting in our own warehouse.

Instead, we work with a local partner installer who carries out the technical quoting, compliance paperwork and physical installation. You still get one point of contact throughout — us — but the advice and the install are handled by two different parties with two different incentives.
